Place these two files here (download once, then they are served locally/offline):
1. qrcode.js  - 'qrcode-generator' by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT), the single-file build that defines global qrcode()
2. jsQR.js    - jsQR by Cosmo Wolfe (Apache-2.0), dist/jsQR.js, defines global jsQR()
Android Chrome has a built-in QR decoder (BarcodeDetector), so jsQR is mainly needed for Windows/desktop/Firefox/Safari.
